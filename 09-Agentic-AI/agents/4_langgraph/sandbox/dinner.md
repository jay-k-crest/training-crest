# Dinner Recommendation: Delice & Sarrasin

**Type:** French · Vegetarian‑friendly (100 % vegan)

## 📍 Address & Contact
- **Location:** 178 West Houston Street (entrance on Bedford), New York, NY 10014
- **Phone:** +1 212‑243‑7200
- **Website:** https://delicesarrasin.com
- **Hours:** Daily 11 AM – 10 PM (bakery opens at 8 AM)

---

## 🍽️ Sample Menu (highlights)
### Appetizers
- **Escargots Beurre Maitre D'hotel** – Oyster‑mushroom “snails” in parsley‑garlic butter (GF‑SF) 
- **Foie Gras Végétal** – Tahini pâté “foie gras” with fig jam (GF‑SF) 
- **Velouté de Carotte** – Carrot soup with pink lentils and garlic butter (GF‑SF) 

### Main Courses
- **Tournedos Rossini** – “Steak” fillet with vegan “foie gras”, truffle‑wine sauce, shaved black truffle ($35)
- **Coq au Vin** – “Chicken” legs braised in red wine with mushrooms & herbs (SF‑NF‑GF)
- **Boeuf Bourguignon** – Vegan “beef” slow‑cooked in red wine (NF‑SF‑GF)
- **Ratatouille Niçoise** – Classic vegetable stew (SF‑GF‑NF)
- **Steak Frites** – Chunk “steak” with black‑truffle French fries (NF)

### Sides & Crepes
- **Truffle Fries**, **Brussels Sprouts**, **Sauteed Spinach**
- **Savory Crepes (Galettes)** – e.g., *Mr. Petrossian* (konjac “salmon”, avocado, dill) 
- **Sweet Crepes** – *La Lolotte* (bananas, dark chocolate, coconut whipped cream), *La Suzette* (flambé orange liqueur), etc.

### Desserts
- **Lavender‑Infused Rice Pudding**, **Orange Crème Brûlée**, **Dark Chocolate Mousse**, **Oat‑Milk Chocolate Cake**

### Drinks
- 100 % vegan wine list (Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, Champagne, etc.)
- Vegan beers, coffees, teas, and non‑alcoholic beverages.
